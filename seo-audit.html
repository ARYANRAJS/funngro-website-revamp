1. Executive Summary

This SEO Audit Report evaluates the digital performance of the Funngro web platform. Funngro serves two target audiences: Teens (14–25 age group) seeking project opportunities and earnings, and Companies & Startups looking to outsource creative tasks to Gen-Z talent. This audit evaluates technical SEO, crawlability, keyword architecture, structured data implementation, and Core Web Vitals performance.

  1. Dynamic JobPosting Schema: Embed JobPosting JSON-LD schema for individual project listings to get rich snippet cards directly inside Google Search.
